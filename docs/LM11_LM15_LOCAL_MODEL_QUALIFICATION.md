**Identity/fencing and protocol:** Five native Tauri commands extend the original `terax-settings.json`/`selfHostedInference` CAS: `self_hosted_profiles_qualification_begin`, `_prepare`, `_acknowledge`, `_cancel`, `_reconcile`. This conservatively prevents requalification during an unchanged epoch as well.

**Outcome/recovery contract and critical flaw:** `rejected` means admission/deadline/process guard failed; `conflict` means native CAS expected snapshot or prepared revision changed; `failed-before-commit` means proposed evidence/schema could not be prepared; `ambiguous` means a native save or its acknowledgement may have crossed the irreversible boundary.
